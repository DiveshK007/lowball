## The rules other builders were rejected for

### Feedback must be a Google Sheet
A `FEEDBACK.md` alone is rejected. The Sheet is the primary artifact — timestamped,
raw, and viewable by anyone with the link. `FEEDBACK.md` summarises and links to it.

- [ ] Sheet created, link sharing set to "anyone with the link can view"
- [ ] Link in `docs/FEEDBACK.md`
- [ ] Link in `VITE_FEEDBACK_FORM_URL` so the app shows it after every bid
- [ ] Opened in an incognito window to confirm it is publicly viewable

### Real users only, with a verifiable tx per user
Every row in `USERS.md` carries a transaction hash the Preprod indexer confirms is
a **`placeBid` on our live contract**. A wallet address alone proves nothing.
`ops/src/verify-users.ts` enforces this and rejects, with a reason: transactions
that do not exist, that touch a different contract, that are on our contract but
are not `placeBid`, and malformed hashes.

> The simulated bidders on `drop-soldout` are **house-run and must never be counted
> as users**. They are contract evidence, labelled as proofs in the gallery, and the
> wallet is ours.

- [ ] `USERS.md` regenerated from the latest Sheet export — never hand-edited
- [ ] Distinct wallet count ≥ 50
- [ ] No house wallet in the list

### No AI-rewritten feedback
Quote responses verbatim, including the awkward and unflattering ones. Summarise in
our words under *themes*; never rewrite theirs.

- [ ] Quotes verbatim, typos included
- [ ] Negative and confused responses kept, not filtered
- [ ] Any paraphrase clearly marked as ours

### Current demo video, real screenshots, no placeholders
- [ ] Video shows multi-drop, wallet picker, sealed bid, privacy panel, reveal, sold-out, receipts
- [ ] README screenshots are real captures of the deployed site
- [ ] No lorem ipsum, no `TODO`, no stock art
