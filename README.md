# LOWBALL 🎰

Provably-fair mystery drops on [Midnight](https://midnight.network). A drop's reserve price is committed onchain before any bid. Bids are sealed forever. Bid at or above the hidden reserve and you win at your price; below, instant refund and nobody ever learns how close you were.

## Idea

Name-your-price gacha, single-player vs the house. The only things ever made public are the things that keep the house honest: the reserve commitment before bids open, and the reserve reveal (hash-verified) after close. Built for the New Moon to Full program — list primitive: Sealed-Bid Auction; category: Consumer focus.
